What to improve

Seven things, in the order they pay off

Money

Find the missing Flex deposits

Flex earned $1,949 across 26 days. Only $1,042 shows up as Amazon deposits in SECU during August. That is a $907 gap, and it is the whole reason the month reads as −$499.

Either it landed after the 31st, or it landed somewhere other than this account. Both are fine answers — not knowing which is not.

DoCheck the first week of September's deposits before treating August as a losing month.
Money

Verizon at $356.56 is the only big lever left

It is 67% of Bills and $4,278 a year. Everything else on the discretionary side is already at or near a record low — Dining Out $33, Online Shopping $183 — so there is nothing left to squeeze there.

Getting it to $250 saves $1,278 a year, more than every optional subscription combined.

DoOne call. Ask what plan the line actually needs and what the loyalty price is.
Data

$3,605 of deposits sit in Transfers and $1,042 of Flex pay sits in Online Shopping. The Pay and Flex B Pay categories exist and have been empty all year.

Until this is fixed, the Online Shopping trend line is half income and the net figure cannot be read straight.

DoRe-file WakeMed → Pay and Amazon deposits → Flex B Pay with "apply to every month" on.

Watch

Bzila had its most expensive month ever

$439.66 cleared the bank, against a Jan–Jun average of $149 — up 195%. On the business ledger it was nine TakeProfit accounts and resets ($835) plus $130 of infrastructure, against $1,195 of CB Edge earnings. Net +$230.

Still positive, so this is a watch item and not a problem. But nine resets in one month is the number to keep an eye on — it is the largest single controllable outflow in the whole month.

DoTrack resets per month next to CB Edge earnings, the way Flex gas is tracked next to Flex pay.
One more thing

Nothing was filed to children in August

That category averaged $165 a month across Jan–Jun and appeared in four of those six months. August is $0. How this was measured
  • Source: budget_statement_tx for 2026-08 via /api/budget/real — what actually cleared, never the register or the plan.
  • Fuel: $370 of Amazon Flex gas is moved out of Sheets/Gas into Flex Gas & Expenses on read, so the month's outflow still totals $5,151.50.
  • Averages divide by the six imported months Jan–Jun, counting a month with no rows in a category as a real zero. July was never imported and is excluded entirely.
  • Two baselines are distorted by a structural gap rather than by behaviour: January has no Rent or Car rows, and Flex gas was only logged from May onward. Rent is therefore reported flat rather than as a delta.
  • Business figures (TakeProfit, CB Edge, infrastructure) come from the Bzila ledger, which is entered by hand and does not always clear the bank in the same month.
