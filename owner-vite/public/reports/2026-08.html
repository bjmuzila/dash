Watch

Bzila had its most expensive month ever

$439.66 cleared the bank against a Jan–Jun average of $149 — up 195%. On the business ledger it was nine TakeProfit accounts and resets ($835) plus $130 of infrastructure, against $1,195 of CB Edge earnings. Net +$230.

Still positive, so this is a watch item and not a problem. But nine resets in one month is the number to keep an eye on — it is the largest single controllable outflow in the whole month.

How this was measured
  • Source: budget_statement_tx for 2026-08 via /api/budget/real — what actually cleared, never the register or the plan.
  • Fuel: $370 of Amazon Flex gas is moved out of Sheets/Gas into Flex Gas & Expenses on read, so the month's outflow still totals $5,151.50.
  • Averages divide by the six imported months Jan–Jun, counting a month with no rows in a category as a real zero. July was never imported and is excluded entirely.
  • Two baselines are distorted by a structural gap rather than by behaviour: January has no Rent or Car rows, and Flex gas was only logged from May onward. Rent is therefore reported flat rather than as a delta.
  • Business figures (TakeProfit, CB Edge, infrastructure) come from the Bzila ledger, which is entered by hand and does not always clear the bank in the same month.
