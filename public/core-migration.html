CORE migration —
Each gold bar is one stay of the CORE at a strike — from the 15-minute slot it arrived to the slot it left — and its weight is how long it held (a hairline is a one-slot blink, a heavy bar held for hours). Nothing joins the bars, so a flip is a gap, not a spike. Recorded levels only: 09:29 open + every 15m to 16:00 ET, written to walls_log when the level moved. Spot is the 5-minute scanner price for each session (the sweep the walls were sampled from); a session without it falls back to the price stamped on each capture. CORE is the single largest |net GEX| strike on the chain; with walls on it stays gold and the other wall is drawn in the colour of the side it currently is (call green / put red) — the same role model the Level Log uses.
