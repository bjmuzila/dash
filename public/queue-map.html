How this is built. Every print lands in exactly one bucket, decided only by size: ≥ cutoff → the three market-maker panels, < cutoff → the two retail panels. At 50 that is 1–49 retail, 50+ market maker. Nothing is counted twice and nothing is left out. The two retail panels are signed contract counts (buy +, sell −). The three MM panels are dollar exposures computed per print with Black–Scholes (r = q = 0), signed by the MM's side and summed per strike: GEX = γ · contracts · 100 · S² · 1% ($M per 1% move) · VEX = vega · contracts · 100 ($K per 1 vol point) · CEX = charm · contracts · 100 · S ($M of delta per day). Where the vol comes from. /proxy/flow-history has no iv column — the live WebSocket frame carries one, but the writer never persists it, so every historical row arrives without it. The vol is therefore solved from the print's own fill price (bisection on the BS price, r = q = 0), which uses only fields history does have: price, strike, spot, expiration. A print whose price sits at or below intrinsic has no solution and falls back to the Fallback IV input. The diagnostic line says how many were solved versus fallen back — a large fallback share means the exposure panels are leaning on an assumption rather than the tape. An MM print that still can't be greeked contributes to no panel; it is not quietly reassigned to retail, and it still counts toward which strikes are shown. Shaded bands are derived, not typed in: the call wall is the strike with the largest net retail call position, the put wall the most negative net retail put, and the pin zone the densest contiguous run of strikes by total contracts.
