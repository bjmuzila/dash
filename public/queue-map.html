How this is built. Prints with size ≥ cutoff are treated as market maker; < cutoff as customer — that's the slider. The two customer panels are signed contract counts (buy +, sell −). The three MM panels are dollar exposures computed per print with Black–Scholes (r = q = 0) from the print's own iv, its days to expiry and the spot at print time, then signed by the MM's side and summed per strike: GEX = γ · contracts · 100 · S² · 1% ($M per 1% move) · VEX = vega · contracts · 100 ($K per 1 vol point) · CEX = charm · contracts · 100 · S ($M of delta per day). Prints with no iv can't be greeked and are counted in the customer panels only — the split readout says how many. Shaded bands are derived, not typed in: the call wall is the strike with the largest net customer call position, the put wall the most negative net customer put, and the pin zone the densest contiguous run of strikes by total contracts.
