Frequently asked questions

Do you offer a trial?

No trial, and no free tier. Monthly is the small commitment: one month, cancelled from your own dashboard whenever you like.

Where does the option data come from?

Institutional option feeds, read as the full chain rather than a sampled slice. Open interest updates daily from OPRA, and quotes stream through the session.

Does CB Edge tell me what to trade?

No, and it is built not to. Every surface describes mechanics and locations: where hedging concentrates, where gamma changes sign, how a named level behaved on its last touches. What you do with that is yours.

What does the graded record actually measure?

Named levels are frozen at the open and marked at the close. A reversal and a coil are graded on touch, so their denominator is touches. The expected move band is graded once per session, on whether the day stayed inside it.

How many tickers do you cover?

The index complex, the megacaps, the daily movers and a long tail beyond them. Boards are added as coverage grows and nothing is removed to make room.

Is there a phone version?

Yes, and it is included. Six screens drawn for a phone rather than a desktop board squeezed down, and alerts land there first.

Do I need options experience to use it?

Every level carries a plain sentence explaining what sits there and why price tends to react to it, so the board reads without the vocabulary first. The learn pages cover the rest.
